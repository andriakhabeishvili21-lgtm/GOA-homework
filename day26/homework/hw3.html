lasha talakhadze

Lasha Talakhadze (Georgian: ლაშა ტალახაძე; Georgian pronunciation: [laʃa tʼalaχadze]; born 2 October 1993) is a Georgian weightlifter, holding the all-time world records independent of weight category in the snatch (225 kg, 496 lb), the clean and jerk (267 kg, 589 lb), and the total (492 kg, 1,085 lb) since 2021.[3] Talakhadze is a three-time Olympic champion,[4] seven-time world champion, and seven-time European champion competing in the super-heavyweight category (105 kg + until 2018[5] and 109 kg + starting in 2018 after the International Weightlifting Federation reorganized the categories).[6] He is a three-time winner of the IWF Male Lifter of the Year.

his achivements


Lasha Talakhadze is a Georgian weightlifter widely regarded as the greatest super-heavyweight (+105kg/+109kg) of all time. His major achievements include winning three Olympic gold medals (2016, 2020, 2024), seven World Championship titles, and seven European Championship titles, while setting over 30 world records throughout his career.
