Thor Odinson, the God of Thunder, is one of Marvel's most powerful and iconic heroes. Created by Stan Lee, Jack Kirby, and Larry Lieber, he first appeared in Journey into Mystery #83 (1962). Core Character Details Origin: The son of Odin (King of Asgard) and Gaea (the Earth Mother). He was raised in the realm of Asgard but has a deep, intrinsic connection to Earth (Midgard). The Lesson of Humility: To teach his arrogant son humility, Odin famously stripped Thor of his memories and powers, banishing him to Earth in the mortal guise of Dr. Donald Blake, a physician with a disabled leg. The Avengers: Thor is a founding member of the Avengers, initially coming together with Iron Man, Hulk, Ant-Man, and Wasp to defeat his trickster brother, Loki. iron man Tony Stark, the armored Avenger known as Iron Man, is a founding member of the Avengers and a central figure in the Marvel Universe. Created by Stan Lee, Larry Lieber, Don Heck, and Jack Kirby, he first appeared in Tales of Suspense #39 (1963).



















Captain America is a mantle currently shared by Steve Rogers, the original Super-Soldier, and Sam Wilson, formerly the Falcon. Originally created in 1941 as a patriotic hero of World War II, the character remains a central pillar of the Marvel Universe in both comics and the Marvel Cinematic Universe (MCU).
