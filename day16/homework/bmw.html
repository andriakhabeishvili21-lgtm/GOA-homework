bmw m4 competition



The BMW M4 Competition is a high-performance sports car featuring a \(3.0\text{-liter}\) twin-turbo inline-six engine producing up to \(530\text{\ hp}\) (\(390\text{\ kW}\)) in xDrive models and \(510\text{\ hp}\) (\(375\text{\ kW}\)) in rear-wheel-drive variants. It boasts a \(0\text{--}100\text{\ km/h}\) time of \(3.5\text{\ seconds}\) for the xDrive, offering exceptional track-focused performance, sharp handling, and a luxurious, tech-forward daily driving experience.
