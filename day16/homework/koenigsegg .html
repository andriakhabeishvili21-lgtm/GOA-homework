koenigsegg regera


The Koenigsegg Regera is a 1,500-hp hybrid hypercar often configured with exposed carbon fiber, including options for colored weaves (blue, red) or the extreme "Koenigsegg Naked Carbon" (KNC) finish. KNC removes all lacquer, reducing weight by 20 kg (44 lbs) and increasing scratch resistance, while all 80 units feature extensive carbon usage, including wheels.
