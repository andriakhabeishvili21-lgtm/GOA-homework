porche 911 gt2 rs



The Porsche 911 GT2 RS is a high-performance, track-focused supercar featuring a 3.8-liter twin-turbo flat-six engine that produces \(700\text{\ hp}\) and \(750\text{\ Nm}\) (\(553\text{\ lb-ft}\)) of torque. As the most powerful 911, it accelerates from \(0-100\text{\ km/h}\) in \(2.8\text{\ seconds}\) with a top speed of \(340\text{\ km/h}\) (\(211\text{\ mph}\)). Key features include rear-wheel drive, extensive carbon fiber, and an optional Weissach package for reduced weight.
